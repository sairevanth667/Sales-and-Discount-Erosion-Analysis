# 📊 Sales Performance & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project is an end-to-end data analytics dashboard built using Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, and the impact of discounts on profitability.

The goal of this project is to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that can support strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance across time, categories, and regions
- Understand customer acquisition and retention patterns
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on revenue and profitability
- Identify top-performing products and customers

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboarding)
- DAX (Calculated Measures & KPIs)
- SQL / Data Modeling Concepts
- Data Cleaning & Transformation

---

### 2️⃣ Customer Acquisition & Retention Analysis
Focuses on customer behavior and lifecycle.

**Key Metrics:**
- New vs Repeat Customers
- Cohort Retention Analysis
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

**Insights:**
- **Repeat customers dominate (~86.8%)**, showing strong retention but lower new acquisition :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}  
- Cohort analysis reveals **drop-offs after initial months**, highlighting retention challenges  
- A few customers contribute disproportionately to revenue (high CLV concentration)

---

### 3️⃣ Sales & Discount Impact Analysis
Analyzes profitability and the effect of discounts.

**Key Metrics:**
- Revenue vs Profit by Category
- Revenue Lost due to Discounts
- Cost vs Profit Funnel

**Insights:**
- Significant revenue loss due to discounting (**~$123K total loss**) :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}  
- Some regions show **negative profit despite strong sales**, indicating inefficient pricing strategies  
- Technology category generates high revenue but profit margins vary significantly  

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights
- Increasing discounts does not always lead to higher profitability  
- Customer retention is strong, but **new customer acquisition needs improvement**  
- Certain states and categories are **loss-making despite high sales volume**  
- Sales growth does not always translate into profit growth  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Optimize discount strategies to protect margins  
- Focus on acquiring new customers through targeted campaigns  
- Identify and fix loss-making regions/products  
- Improve pricing and cost efficiency
